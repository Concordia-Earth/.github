# 🌍 CONCORDIA — Belongs to Our Species

**CONCORDIA** is an open-source, decentralized, and global technological infrastructure designed to allow humanity to express its collective will on decisions affecting our shared future.

---

### 📌 Core Principles
- **CC0 License (Public Domain):** No owners, corporate interests, or profit motives.
- **Self-Sovereign Identity (SSI) & ZKP:** Human uniqueness verification powered by Zero-Knowledge Proofs, ensuring absolute anonymity without storing personal data.
- **Immutability & Transparency:** Public, auditable consensus and proposal logging built on blockchain architecture.
- **Resilience & Integrity:** Self-healing design resilient against capture, censorship, or manipulation attempts.
