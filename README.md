# 🌍 CONCORDIA — Belongs to Our Species

**CONCORDIA** is an open-source, decentralized, and global technological infrastructure designed to allow humanity to express its collective will on decisions affecting our shared future.

---

### 📌 Core Principles
- **CC0 License (Public Domain):** No owners, corporate interests, or profit motives.
- **Self-Sovereign Identity (SSI) & ZKP:** Human uniqueness verification powered by Zero-Knowledge Proofs, ensuring absolute anonymity without storing personal data.
- **Immutability & Transparency:** Public, auditable consensus and proposal logging built on blockchain architecture.
- **Resilience & Integrity:** Self-healing design resilient against capture, censorship, or manipulation attempts.

## 🏛️ Governance & Legal Roadmap

CONCORDIA is an open-source, decentralized, global public-good project designed under a strictly non-profit philosophy.

To ensure the inalienability of its core mission, operational transparency, and long-term protection for all community members and contributors, the project observes the following governance framework:

### Phase 0: Seed & Initial Development (Current Stage)
- **Management:** Voluntarily maintained by the initial founders and open-source contributors.
- **Protection:** Covered by open-source licensing with explicit legal disclaimers protecting developers and maintainers from personal liability.

### Phase 1: Community Consolidation
- **Management:** Decision-making transitions toward a technical and ethical committee formed by active core contributors.
- **Legal Structure:** Establishment of a formal **Non-Profit Association**. This legal entity will hold its own corporate personality and act as the neutral custodian of the `concordia.earth` domain, official repositories, and technical infrastructure—separating legal liability from the personal assets of individual contributors.

### Phase 2: Institutionalization & Open Protocol
- **Management:** Transition to a fully distributed governance model (DAO / Foundation).
- **Legal Structure:** Transfer of all association assets to a dedicated **Foundation** tasked exclusively with preserving protocol integrity, community treasury management, and global ecosystem growth.
